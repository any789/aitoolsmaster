Google I/O 2026: Everything Google Announced for AI

May 13, 2026·~2,000 words

Google I/O 2026 was AI-heavy, as expected. Here are the announcements that actually matter for anyone building or using AI tools.

1. Gemini 3.1 Pro: Smarter, Cheaper

Google's flagship model won 13 of 16 major benchmarks in Q1 2026 and costs roughly one-third of competing models. The deep Workspace integration makes it the best choice for anyone already in Google's ecosystem. Natural language spreadsheet generation hit 70% success rate on complex tasks.

2. NotebookLM: Audio Overviews Go Interactive

NotebookLM's biggest update lets you interrupt the AI-generated podcast to ask follow-up questions in real time. The AI answers strictly from your uploaded documents. This is the best tool for digesting large document sets with zero hallucination risk.

3. Veo 2 Opens to More Users

Google's video generation model Veo 2 expanded access. It produces 4K video with realistic physics and motion. The quality gap between Veo 2 and competitors like Runway and Sora has widened significantly.

4. Google Veo: AI Video at 4K

Veo now generates 4K video with consistent physics and minimal artifacts. It is the best AI video tool for realistic footage. Access is still limited but expanding.

5. Workspace AI Gets Proactive

Gemini in Google Workspace can now auto-generate documents by pulling context from your emails, files, chats, and calendar events. You ask for a project brief and it assembles everything without you specifying sources.

What This Means

Google is betting that deep integration — AI that lives inside the tools you already use — will beat standalone AI assistants. For users, the best AI tool may no longer be the smartest model, but the one that fits most seamlessly into your existing workflow.
