AI note-taking has moved from gimmick to essential in 2026. The tools here genuinely save hours per week — if you pick the right one for your workflow. We tested each across real meetings, noisy environments, and multi-speaker conversations.

1. Jamie — Best Overall for Professionals

Rating: 9.0/10

Jamie is the tool we kept coming back to. It joins your calendar and processes meetings without a bot — no awkward "Fireflies is joining" messages. The summaries are structured by topic with speaker quotes and clear action items.

What we loved:

  • Bot-free — nothing joins your call
  • Speaker memory learns voices over time
  • GDPR compliant with EU servers
  • Audio permanently deleted after processing
  • 100+ language support

What could improve:

  • No video recording
  • Free plan caps at 10 meetings (30 min each)

Best for: Professionals who want notes without clients seeing a bot join.

Pricing: Free (10 meetings/mo), Plus €25/mo, Pro €47/mo.

2. Otter AI — Best for Team Collaboration

Rating: 8.5/10

Otter is the veteran here. Its live transcription works well with Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams. The team features make it a solid choice for small teams.

What we loved: Live transcription, Otter Chat for searching past meetings, Slack and Salesforce integrations.

What could improve: Speaker ID can be inconsistent, no offline mode, limited to 4 languages.

Pricing: Basic $0, Pro $16.99/user/mo, Business $30/user/mo.

3. Fireflies.ai — Best for Searchable Archives

Rating: 8.0/10

Fireflies excels at making past meetings searchable. The CRM integrations are the deepest of any tool here.

Pricing: Free (800 min lifetime), Pro $18/user/mo, Business $29/user/mo.

4. Krisp — Best Audio Quality

Rating: 8.5/10

Krisp started as noise cancellation and added transcription. Crystal clear audio in any environment.

Pricing: Free trial, Core $16/user/mo, Advanced $30/user/mo.

5. Superpowered — Best for Privacy

Rating: 8.0/10

No bot, no recordings stored, SOC-2 certified. Ideal for legal, finance, healthcare.

Pricing: Free with limits, paid from $20/mo.

6. Fathom — Best Unlimited Free Plan

Rating: 9.5/10

Fathom offers unlimited free transcription with no caps. Works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams.

Pricing: Free (unlimited individual), Team $19/user/mo.

Final Verdict

Our pick: For most professionals, Jamie offers the best balance of quality, privacy, and value. If you need unlimited free, Fathom is the surprising winner.
