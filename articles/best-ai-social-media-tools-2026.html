Vista Social — Best Free Tier

9.0/10 — Vista Social offers a genuinely useful free plan: 3 social profiles, unlimited scheduling, and AI content suggestions. The AI caption generator is surprisingly good.

Buffer — Best for Small Teams

8.5/10 — Buffer added AI features without losing its simplicity. The AI suggests post variations and optimal posting times based on audience data.

Later — Best Visual Planning

8.5/10 — Later's visual content calendar is the best for Instagram. AI suggests hashtags and captions that match your brand voice.
