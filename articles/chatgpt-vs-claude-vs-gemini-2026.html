In 2026, all three major AI assistants are excellent. The differences come down to specific strengths rather than overall quality.

Claude — Best for Deep Work

9.5/10 — Claude excels at complex reasoning, long-form writing, and code analysis. The 200K context window means it can process entire books.

ChatGPT — Best All-Rounder

9.0/10 — ChatGPT is the most versatile. GPT-4o handles text, images, and code equally well. The plugin ecosystem is the largest.

Gemini — Best Google Integration

8.5/10 — Gemini deeply integrates with Google Workspace. The 1M context window is unmatched.

Final Verdict

Claude for complex tasks. ChatGPT for everyday use. Gemini if you live in Google's ecosystem.
