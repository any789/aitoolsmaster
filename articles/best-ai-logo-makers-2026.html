Our Testing Experience

We designed logos for five different business types — a coffee shop, a tech startup, a law firm, a fitness brand, and an e-commerce store — to test how each tool handles different industries. Looka impressed us with its style quiz upfront; it asked detailed questions about vibe, colors, and icons, and generated logos that matched the brief surprisingly well. The full brand kit with business cards and social templates was a genuine time-saver for the coffee shop brand.

Brandmark produced the cleanest minimalist designs — its algorithm favors simplicity, and the law firm logo came out looking like a real agency had designed it. However, customization was limited; you get a few variations and that's it. LogoAI generated more varied concepts than any other tool, but quality control was inconsistent — about 30% of suggestions were unusable. Hatchful (Shopify's free tool) was surprisingly capable for a free option; the coffee shop logo was decent, though we couldn't upload custom fonts. Logo.com was the fastest option at under 2 minutes from start to download, but the designs were generic and lacked the polish of Looka or Brandmark.
