Our Test Experience with AI Email Assistants

We spent two weeks testing each AI email assistant with real inboxes containing over 200 messages per account. For Shortwave, the AI summaries were surprisingly accurate — it correctly identified action items from a 40-message thread about a product launch. Superhuman AI felt fastest for triage: the keyboard shortcuts combined with AI-powered snippets let us process 50 emails in under 8 minutes. Ellie impressed us with how naturally it matched our writing style; replies needed almost no editing after the first day of training. Mailbutler offers the most features per dollar but the AI feels less polished than dedicated tools. Missive AI shines for teams — we used it to collaboratively draft a client proposal across three team members without leaving the inbox. On average, AI email assistants saved us about 4.5 hours per week compared to manual email management.
