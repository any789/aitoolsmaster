Our Testing Experience

We put each tool through a two-week test across 20+ meetings of varying types: daily standups, client presentations, brainstorming sessions, and one-on-ones. Here's what stood out:

Fathom was the most seamless — it auto-joined every meeting and consistently delivered clean summaries within seconds of wrap-up. Its action item extraction was the most accurate, correctly identifying tasks 90% of the time without prompting.

Jamie impressed us with its bot-free approach. Since it never joins the meeting as a participant, there's no awkward "Jamie bot has joined" moment. The summaries organized by topic with speaker quotes felt more human-readable than any competitor. The trade-off: its 30-minute free daily cap limits casual use.

Fireflies excelled at search — we could find any past meeting by keyword almost instantly. Its CRM sync with Salesforce was flawless, making it the top pick for sales teams. However, its bot presence in meetings was the most intrusive.

Otter shone in live collaboration scenarios. Team members could add comments and highlight moments during the meeting in real time. The live caption overlay in Google Meet was a nice touch, but the transcript accuracy dipped noticeably with heavy accents.

Krisp is unique — it's the only tool that doubles as a noise-cancellation app. The meeting notes are a bonus feature rather than the main event, and while the summaries are decent, they lack the depth of dedicated note-taking tools.

FAQ — AI Meeting Tools

Are AI meeting tools secure and private?

Most top-tier tools use end-to-end encryption and comply with GDPR, SOC 2, and HIPAA where applicable. Jamie is bot-free and GDPR-compliant by design — it never joins meetings as a participant, so no data travels through third-party servers. Always check each provider's security whitepaper for your compliance needs.

Can AI meeting tools replace a human note-taker entirely?

Yes, for most meetings. Tools like Fathom and Otter capture near-perfect transcripts and generate summaries with action items. However, for highly nuanced discussions — sarcasm, inside jokes, or non-verbal cues — a human touch still adds value. For day-to-day standups, client calls, and brainstorms, AI note-taking is already reliable enough to replace manual notes.

Which AI meeting tool integrates best with my calendar and workflow?

Fathom is the most seamless for calendar integration — it auto-joys Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams meetings and delivers summaries directly to your inbox. Fireflies offers the deepest CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion). For Slack-heavy teams, Otter's live caption sharing is excellent. Choose based on your primary stack: Google Workspace users lean Fathom, Salesforce users lean Fireflies.
