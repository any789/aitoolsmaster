Our Testing Experience

We tested each headshot generator with a group of five volunteers of different skin tones, ages, and face shapes. Each volunteer uploaded 15 selfies taken on an iPhone under natural light. HeadshotPro produced the most consistent results across all skin tones — the lighting and skin texture looked natural in over 90% of outputs, and the outfit-changing feature actually worked well, swapping shirts and jackets with minimal edge artifacts.

Try It On AI generated the most variety per dollar, giving each volunteer over 200 headshots in 20 minutes. However, about 15-20% had noticeable artifacts around glasses and facial hair. StudioShot excelled at corporate settings — its indoor office backgrounds were the most realistic, though turnaround was slower at 40 minutes. ProHeadshot was quickest (15 minutes) but offered fewer backgrounds and no outfit changes, making it best for simple LinkedIn-style shots. Section handled team uploads seamlessly — we uploaded headshots for a 10-person team and got consistent lighting and backgrounds across all members.
