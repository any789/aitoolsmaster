## 1. Jamie — Best Overall for Professionals

**Rating: ⭐ 9.0/10**

Jamie is the tool we kept coming back to. It joins your calendar and processes meetings without a bot — no awkward "Fireflies is joining" messages. The summaries are structured by topic with speaker quotes and clear action items.

**What we loved:**
- Bot-free — nothing joins your call
- Speaker memory learns voices over time
- GDPR compliant with EU servers
- Audio permanently deleted after processing
- 100+ language support, handles multilingual meetings automatically

**What could improve:**
- No video recording
- Free plan caps at 10 meetings and 30 minutes each

**Best for:** Professionals who want accurate notes without their clients seeing a bot join.

**Pricing:** Free (10 meetings/mo, 30 min cap), Plus €25/mo (20 meetings, 2hr), Pro €47/mo (unlimited, 3hr), Team €39/seat/mo.

## 2. Otter AI — Best for Team Collaboration

**Rating: ⭐ 8.5/10**

Otter is the veteran here. Its live transcription works well with Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams. The team features — shared workspaces, comments, and reactions — make it a solid choice for small teams.

**What we loved:**
- Live transcription with speaker identification
- Otter Chat lets you search past meetings conversationally
- Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot integrations

**What could improve:**
- Speaker ID is inconsistent — especially with accents
- No offline mode
- Limited to English, Spanish, French, Japanese

**Best for:** Teams that already use Zoom/Teams and want collaborative notes.

**Pricing:** Basic $0, Pro $16.99/user/mo, Business $30/user/mo.

## 3. Fireflies.ai — Best for Searchable Archives

**Rating: ⭐ 8.0/10**

Fireflies excels at making past meetings searchable. Type "what did we decide about pricing" and it finds the exact moment. The CRM integrations are the deepest of any tool here.

**What we loved:**
- Smart search across all past meetings
- Deep CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Soundbites — share short clips from meetings

**What could improve:**
- Bot feels intrusive — visible joining message
- Free plan is very limited (800 min lifetime)
- Extra AI credits needed for some features

**Best for:** Sales teams that need CRM-connected meeting intelligence.

**Pricing:** Free (800 min lifetime), Pro $18/user/mo, Business $29/user/mo.

## 4. Krisp — Best Audio Quality

**Rating: ⭐ 8.5/10**

Krisp started as noise cancellation and added transcription. The result: crystal clear audio in any environment, with solid AI notes as a bonus. If you take calls from coffee shops or coworking spaces, this is the one.

**What we loved:**
- Best noise cancellation of any tool tested
- Unlimited transcripts for paid users
- No bot joins meetings

**What could improve:**
- More complex to set up than competitors
- Stores audio recordings on servers
- 16 languages on Core plan (95+ in beta)

**Best for:** People who take calls from noisy environments.

**Pricing:** Free trial, Core $16/user/mo, Advanced $30/user/mo.

## 5. Superpowered — Best for Privacy

**Rating: ⭐ 8.0/10**

Superpowered is built for the privacy-conscious. No bot, no recordings stored, and SOC-2 Type-2 certified. It captures audio from your device and generates clean notes without leaving a trace.

**What we loved:**
- Complete privacy — no data stored on servers
- 50+ language support
- GDPR and SOC-2 compliant
- Integrates with Slack, Notion, Google Drive, Salesforce

**What could improve:**
- Limited device support (macOS, Windows only — no mobile apps)
- Short storage window before cleanup

**Best for:** Legal, finance, healthcare professionals with strict compliance requirements.

**Pricing:** Free with limits, paid plans start at $20/mo.

## 6. Tactiq — Best Free Tier for Individuals

**Rating: ⭐ 7.5/10**

Tactiq is a Chrome extension that transcribes Google Meet calls in real time. It is simple, lightweight, and the free tier is genuinely usable — 10 meetings per month with full transcripts.

**What we loved:**
- Simple Chrome extension — no setup
- Real-time captions during meetings
- Copy highlights with one click

**What could improve:**
- Google Meet only — no Zoom or Teams support
- Transcripts are raw, less polished than Jamie or Otter
- No speaker memory

**Best for:** Google Meet users who want free, simple transcription.

**Pricing:** Free (10 meetings/mo), Pro $10/mo (unlimited).

## 7. Fathom — Best Unlimited Free Plan

**Rating: ⭐ 9.5/10**

Fathom is the dark horse of AI note-taking. It offers unlimited free transcription with no caps — something no other major player does. The AI summaries are clean, the interface is fast, and it works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams.

**What we loved:**
- Completely free with unlimited recordings
- Clean, fast interface
- AI summaries with action items
- Works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams

**What could improve:**
- Team features cost $19/user/mo
- No offline mode
- Less well-known — smaller community

**Best for:** Anyone who wants unlimited free meeting notes. At zero cost, it is hard to beat.

**Pricing:** Free (unlimited individual), Team $19/user/mo.

## Final Verdict

There is no single best AI note-taking tool — it depends entirely on your use case.

- **If you want unlimited free:** Fathom. No cap, no catch. It is genuinely excellent for individual use.
- **If you need bot-free, professional notes:** Jamie. The quality of summaries is noticeably better, and clients never see a bot join.
- **If you work in noisy environments:** Krisp. The noise cancellation is transformative, and the transcription is a bonus.
- **If compliance is non-negotiable:** Superpowered. SOC-2 and GDPR with no stored recordings.
- **If you need deep CRM integration:** Fireflies. The search and CRM hooks are unmatched.
- **If you are a Google Meet user on a budget:** Tactiq. Simple, free, and does the job.

**Our pick:** For most professionals, **Jamie** offers the best balance of quality, privacy, and value. If you need unlimited free, **Fathom** is the surprising winner.
