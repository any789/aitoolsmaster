## Why I Built This

Two reasons.

**First:** Most "AI review sites" are affiliate farms. They rank tools by commission rate, not by quality. I wanted one place where the reviews actually tell the truth — including when a tool is overhyped or not worth the price.

**Second:** "Free" shouldn't mean "bring your own API key." If a tool asks for your OpenAI key, it's not free. I built tools that genuinely require nothing — open the page, use them, close the page.
